Why we built Rasin

Freelancers lose hours every month sorting through confusing bank statements, dreading quarterly tax deadlines, and worrying about unexpected penalties.

Most modern financial tools have rushed to add black-box AI that silently changes numbers and leaves no paper trail you can check.

We built Rasin to keep AI on a short leash. The AI suggests, you approve, and the accounting and tax math is plain code that always balances.
